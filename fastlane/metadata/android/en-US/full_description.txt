Nuvora is a free and open-source (GPL-3.0-or-later) Android application aimed at translating meteorological data into clear, honest, and easy-to-understand information.

Provisional metadata (Milestone 0):
- The project is currently in its initial foundation stage (version 0.0.1).
- Currently includes the base Jetpack Compose + Material 3 user interface and English/Spanish localization skeleton.
- Weather forecasts, Open-Meteo integration, and location management are under active development for upcoming milestones and are not yet available in this build.
- Privacy-respecting: no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no permissions required in this initial version.
