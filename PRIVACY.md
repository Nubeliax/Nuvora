# Privacy Policy

Nuvora is built with privacy by design and contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or proprietary tracking SDKs.

## Current State (Milestone 0)
In its current foundation stage (version `0.0.1`), Nuvora:
- Requests **no Android permissions** (not even `INTERNET` or location permissions).
- Makes **no network requests** and collects, stores, or transmits **zero personal or device data**.
- Disables cleartext network traffic (`android:usesCleartextTraffic="false"`).

## Future Milestones
When weather features are introduced in future milestones (using Open-Meteo), this document will be updated before release to transparently describe any network requests, coordinates handling, and local storage involved.
